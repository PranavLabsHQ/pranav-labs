# Writing Principles

## Clarity First

If a sentence can be shorter, shorten it.

Bad

> We leverage cutting-edge artificial intelligence technologies to revolutionize your workflow.

Good

> We build AI systems that automate repetitive work.

---

## Be Specific

Never say

> Better software.

Instead say

> AI-powered customer support.

> Internal business dashboards.

> Workflow automation.

Specific creates trust.

---

## Explain Simply

Assume the reader is smart.

Do not assume they know technical terms.

Explain without sounding simplistic.

---

## Use Plain English

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Instead of

> Utilize

Write

> Use

Instead of

> Commence

Write

> Start

Instead of

> Facilitate

Write

> Help

---

## Respect the Reader

Never waste words.

Never repeat yourself.

Every sentence should provide value.

# Messaging

Always focus on outcomes.

Not technology.

Wrong

> Built with Next.js.

Better

> Loads fast on every device.

Wrong

> Uses AI.

Better

> Reduces repetitive work.

Technology supports the message.

It is not the message.

---

# Headlines

Headlines should be short.

Examples

Build What's Next.

Software Built to Last.

Automation That Works.

Engineering Over Complexity.

Modern Software.

Real Business Impact.

Avoid

The Future of AI-Powered Digital Transformation

---

# Paragraphs

Maximum

3–5 lines.

Large walls of text reduce readability.

---

# Lists

Prefer bullets over long paragraphs.

Keep lists short.

---

# Buttons

Short.

Action-oriented.

Examples

Book a Call

Explore Our Work

View Projects

Read More

Contact Us

Avoid

Learn More About Our Amazing Solutions

Continue

Clickbait.
