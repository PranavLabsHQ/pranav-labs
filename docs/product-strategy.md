# Pranav Labs Product Strategy

> Version: 1.0
> Last Updated: September 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines what Pranav Labs builds, who we build for, and how we make product decisions.

Every new product, service, feature, or opportunity should be evaluated against this strategy.

If it doesn't align, we don't build it.

---

# Mission

Build software that solves real-world problems through engineering, AI, and automation.

We create products that save time, increase productivity, and enable businesses and developers to do more with less effort.

---

# Vision

Become an engineering-first software company recognized for building reliable AI products, automation systems, developer tools, and modern business software.

Our goal is not to build the most products.

Our goal is to build products people genuinely rely on.

---

# Long-Term Direction

Pranav Labs will evolve through three phases.

---

## Phase 1 — Engineering Studio (Current)

Generate revenue by building high-quality software for clients.

Focus:

- Business websites
- AI automation
- Internal tools
- Landing pages
- Business software

Goal:

Generate cash flow while developing engineering capability and building a strong reputation.

---

## Phase 2 — Product Studio

Launch reusable software products.

Examples:

- SaaS applications
- AI tools
- Developer utilities
- Productivity software
- Business platforms

Goal:

Recurring revenue.

---

## Phase 3 — Product Company

Become product-first.

Services continue only when they support product growth.

Primary revenue:

- SaaS subscriptions
- APIs
- AI platforms
- Developer products

# Decision Framework

Before building anything, ask:

1.

Does this solve a real problem?

2.

Would someone pay for it?

3.

Can we maintain it?

4.

Can it scale?

5.

Does it align with our mission?

If the answer is "no" to most of these, don't build it.

---

# Service Strategy

Services are not the destination.

They fund product development.

Client work should:

- strengthen engineering skills
- generate case studies
- create relationships
- finance future products

---

# Open Source Strategy

Open source is part of the brand.

- teach
- help developers
- improve workflows
- demonstrate engineering excellence

# Product Quality Standard

Before release, every product should be:

- Functional
- Tested
- Documented
- Fast
- Accessible
- Maintainable

Shipping quickly is good.

Shipping carelessly is not.
